About

I am a Research Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer committed to creating impactful, data-driven solutions. My work bridges rigorous scientific inquiry with practical engineering, transforming complex data into actionable insights and robust, deployed systems. What I do:

Measure. I design and implement the data infrastructure necessary for rigorous evaluation, defining key metrics, designing trials, and building robust systems to collect and manage large-scale measurements of both experimental and observational data.

Explain. I discover actionable insights and make evidence-based recommendations through comprehensive analytics and statistical analyses. My expertise spans a variety of techniques, including Bayesian inference, longitudinal analysis, and causal inference.

Predict. I use advanced machine learning and probabilistic forecasting techniques to deliver accurate, tailored predictions with transparent, quantified uncertainty. My domain expertise includes specialized areas such as environmental and agricultural forecasting with physics-informed neural networks, as well as computer vision, facial recognition and natural language processing.

Build. I engineer end-to-end solutions, encompassing everything from scalable backend systems and process automation to intuitive analytics dashboards and user-facing applications.
