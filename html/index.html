About Us

Standardizing care. Streamlining delivery.

Community health workers treat millions of children for malnutrition and infectious disease every year — often with paper forms, limited supervision, and no feedback on outcomes.

Last Mile Science builds the digital backbone for community health programs: mobile tools that standardize treatment protocols, dashboards that give supervisors real-time visibility, and analytics that turn program data into actionable intelligence.

What We Build

Our Solutions

phone_android

Mobile App for CHWs

Guides health workers through malnutrition screening (MUAC, edema checks), treatment protocols (CMAM/IMAM), and follow-up scheduling — with offline support for low-connectivity areas.

dashboard

Dashboard for Managers

Real-time visibility into treatment coverage, default rates, supply levels, and CHW activity — so supervisors can identify problems early and target support where it's needed.

hub

Deployment & Analytics

AI models that predict supply needs, flag at-risk cases for follow-up, and identify geographic coverage gaps — turning routine data into program intelligence.
